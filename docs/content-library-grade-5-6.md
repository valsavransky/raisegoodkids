# Content Library: 5th & 6th Grade (ages 10–11)
*Companion to screens-and-flows.md. Starting content set for Expected & Gigs setup (screen 7). Meant to be tested and revised with real families before it's treated as final — not a finished, authoritative list. Last updated: Sept 23, 2026.*

The third grade band authored, alongside `content-library-grade-3-4.md` and `content-library-grade-k-2.md`. Covers 5th and 6th grade as one band, same reasoning as the other bands: the real variable across two adjacent grades is how much independence has kicked in, not a different set of tasks entirely.

**A few tasks graduate from paid Gig to unpaid Expected at this band.** Taking the trash/recycling to the curb was a Quick Gig in 3rd/4th; here it becomes a weekly Expected item, since a 10-11-year-old handling it without being asked (and without getting paid for it) is a realistic step toward the "Expected is just part of being in the family" framing the whole app is built around. Same logic for packing their own lunch, which was a K-2nd Quick Gig ("help pack their lunch box") and becomes a daily Expected item here. This is a deliberate pattern worth carrying forward into future bands, not a one-off.

## Notes for whoever expands this next
- **The "Gig graduates to Expected" pattern introduced here** (trash/recycling, packing lunch) should keep showing up in future bands — it's a concrete, kid-legible way to teach that responsibilities grow with capability, and it's a nice contrast to Gigs, which should mostly *add* new, bigger options rather than just recycle the same list with higher effort tiers.
- **First supervised-stove and supervised-mower items** appear in this band (`Cook a simple stovetop meal`, `Mow the lawn with a push mower`) — both explicitly flagged "supervised" in the name itself, not just in this doc's notes, since the item name is what actually renders in the app. Worth keeping that convention (safety caveat baked into the visible label, not just documentation) as later bands introduce less-supervised versions of the same tasks.
- Still avoiding anything that reads as "gig economy outside the home" (a neighbor's yard, an errand alone in public) — out of scope per the vision doc ("teens/independent spenders, community/neighborhood gig marketplace participants" explicitly excluded from v1). Everything here stays inside the household.
- Same open item as every other band: needs real-family testing before any of it is treated as settled, and effort tiers are always parent-overridable.
