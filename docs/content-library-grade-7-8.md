# Content Library: 7th & 8th Grade (ages 12–13)
*Companion to screens-and-flows.md. Starting content set for Expected & Gigs setup (screen 7). Meant to be tested and revised with real families before it's treated as final — not a finished, authoritative list. Last updated: Sept 23, 2026.*

The fourth grade band authored, alongside `content-library-grade-k-2.md`, `content-library-grade-3-4.md`, and `content-library-grade-5-6.md`. Covers 7th and 8th grade as one band. This is the oldest band in the current design target (see screens-and-flows.md's open item — high school content is still unauthored) and the most independent: most Gigs here can be done solo, without a parent supervising in the room, unlike every younger band.

**Continues the "Gig graduates to Expected" pattern from 5th/6th.** Doing a full load of laundry start-to-finish — wash, dry, fold, put away — was a Big Job Gig through 5th/6th; here it becomes a weekly Expected item. Managing their own screen-time limit (rather than a parent enforcing "homework before screen time" as an external rule) is new at this band, reflecting that a 12-13-year-old is closer to self-regulating than being regulated.

## Notes for whoever expands this next
- **"Solo" language replaces "help"/"supervised" throughout this band's Gigs** — car washing, meal prep, mowing, and windows all drop their younger bands' supervision qualifiers. That's the core shape of this band: same tasks as 5th/6th mostly, minus the training wheels, not a wholesale new task list.
- **Deliberately stayed inside the household** — no errands outside the home, no neighbor's yard, no unsupervised outings, even though a realistic 12-13-year-old could plausibly do some of that. Per the vision doc, "teens/independent spenders, community/neighborhood gig marketplace participants" are explicitly out of scope for v1; a parent can always add a custom item if their own family is comfortable with that, but it shouldn't be a default suggestion this app makes on their behalf.
- **High school content (9th-12th) is still unauthored** — this is the oldest band in the current app design target. If/when the app extends further, it'll need its own band rather than assuming 7th/8th's list just scales up again, same lesson learned from K-2nd not being "an easier 3rd/4th."
- Same open item as every other band: needs real-family testing before any of it is treated as settled, and effort tiers are always parent-overridable.
